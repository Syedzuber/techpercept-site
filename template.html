II

The process. The same lead, the same road — with a system running through it.

This is what runs today at a 400+ MW EPC and RESCO developer in Delhi, built in the order the problems showed up. Three layers assemble around the road as you scroll.

Layer 1 · Revenue · Zoho CRM · Phase 1
1
FIRST CONTACT · 21:05
Captured into Zoho in seconds, deduplicated, source-tagged. The prospect gets a WhatsApp reply with a name, a sizing question and a slot for tomorrow. Routed to the right rep by city and system size.
2
THE QUOTE · DAY 1 → DAY 14
Proposal generated from a template the same day — sizing, subsidy, payback, filled from the CRM. The cadence fires: a subsidy explainer on day 3, a call on day 6, a site visit on day 10. Order signed on touch five. The dashboard logs it.
Layer 2 · Operations · Custom-built · Phase 2
3
HANDOVER · DAY 0
Project auto-created from the deal — site, sizing, price, contacts carried over. Ops picks up a complete brief. Zoho's project module couldn't model this, so we built a platform that could: survey → design → procurement → inventory → SCM → execution → QA/QC → finance → handover.
4
EXECUTION & BILLING · DAY 12 → DAY 83
DISCOM delay flagged on day 12 on the CEO dashboard, not day 41. A project-health score names what's blocked, by how many days, and why. QA sign-off triggers the milestone invoice automatically. Paid. Retention scheduled. Everyone from the site engineer to the CEO sees their own view of it.
Layer 3 · Field · Mobile apps · Phase 3
5
ON THE ROOF
Survey and execution teams aren't at desks. Site data — surveys, progress, photos, sign-offs — captured on the roof, in the platform the same minute. Not in a WhatsApp group nobody can search.
